# Remote Work
Employees may request remote work through their manager. Team members should keep their calendars updated and protect confidential company information when working outside the office.

## Leave Requests
Planned leave should be submitted through the internal leave portal at least five working days in advance when practical. Urgent or sick leave should be reported to the manager as soon as possible.

## Equipment
Company laptops should use approved security updates and screen locks. Lost devices must be reported to the IT help desk promptly.

## Document Retention
Project records should be stored in the approved company workspace. Personal copies of confidential files should not be kept after the business need ends.
