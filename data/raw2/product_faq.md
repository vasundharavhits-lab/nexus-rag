# Account Access
Users can reset a forgotten password from the sign-in page by selecting “Forgot password.” A reset link is sent to the email address registered to the account.

## Data Export
Workspace administrators can request a CSV export from Settings > Data Management. Large exports may take several minutes to prepare.

## Search Behavior
Search supports exact phrases, related concepts, and filters for document status and date. Search results may include short excerpts to help users identify relevant passages.

## Support
If an export fails repeatedly, users should record the approximate time and request ID before contacting support. They should not send passwords or authentication codes to support staff.
