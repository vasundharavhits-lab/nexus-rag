I wish to see You adorned in the same
way with a diadem on the head, and holding
a mace and a discus in two of Your hands.
O Lord with a thousand arms, O Universal
Being, appear again in the same four-armed
Form. (46)

Sri Bhagavan said: Arjuna! pleased with
you I have shown you, through My own power
of Yoga, this supreme, effulgent, primal and
infinite Cosmic Body, which was never seen
before by any else than you. (47)
