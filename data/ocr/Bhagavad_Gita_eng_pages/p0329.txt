Dying when Rajas predominates, he is
born among those attached to action; even so
the man who has expired during the
preponderance of Tamas is reborn in the species
of stupid creatures, such as insects, and beasts
etc. (15)

The reward of a righteous act, they say, is
Sattvika and faultless (in the shape of joy,
wisdom and dispassion etc.); sorrow is declared
to be the fruit of a Rajasika act and ignorance,
the fruit of a Tamasika act. (16)
