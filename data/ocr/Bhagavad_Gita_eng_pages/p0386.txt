(Prohibited acts and those that are motivated
by desire should no doubt be given up.) But it
is not advisable to abandon a prescribed duty.

Its abandonment through ignorance has been
declared as Tamasika. (7)

Should anyone give up his duties for fear
of physical strain, thinking that all action is

verily of the nature of discomfort,—practising
such Rajasika form of renunciation, he reaps

not the fruit renunciation. (8)
