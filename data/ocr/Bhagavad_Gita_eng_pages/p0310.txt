It is incomprehensible; it is close at hand
stand afar too. (15)

Though integral like sapce in its undivided
aspect. It appears divided as it were in all
animate and inanimate beings. And that godhead,
which is the only object worth knowing,
is the sustainer of beings (as Visnu), the destroyer

(as Rudra) and the creator of all (as
Brahma). (16)
