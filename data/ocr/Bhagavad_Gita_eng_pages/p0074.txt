From anger arises infatuation; from
infatuation, confusion ofmemory; from confusion
of memory, loss of reason; and from loss of
reason one goes to complete ruin. (63)

But the self-controlled practicant, while
enjoying the various sense-objects through
his senses, which are disciplined and free

from likes and dislikes, attains placidity of
mind. (64)
