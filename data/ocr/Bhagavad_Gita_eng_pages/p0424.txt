Safijaya said: Thus I heard the mysterious
and thrilling conversation between Sri Krsna
and the high-souled Arjuna, son of Kunti.(74)

Having been blessed with the divine vision
by the grace of Sri Vyasa, I heard this supremely

esoteric gospel from the Lord of Yoga, Sri |

Krsna Himself, imparting it to Arjuna before
my very eyes. (75)
