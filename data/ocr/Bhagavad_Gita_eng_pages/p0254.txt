I am Krsna among the Vrsnis, Arjuna
among the sons of Pandu, Vyasa among the
_ Sages, and the sage Sukracarya among the
wise. (37)

I am the subduing power in rulers; I am
righteousness in those who seek to conquer.
Of things to be kept secret, Iam the custodian

in the shape of reticence; and Iam the wisdom
of the wise. (38)
