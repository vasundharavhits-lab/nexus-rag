Arjuna, the Vedas thus deal with evolutes
of the three Gunas (modes of Prakriti); viz.,
worldly enjoyments and the means of attaining
such enjoyments; be thou indifferent to these
enjoyments and their means, rising above
pairs of opposites like pleasur and pain etc.,
established in the Eternal Existence (God),
absolutely unconcerned about the supply of
wants and the preservation of what has been
already attained, and self-controlled. (45)
