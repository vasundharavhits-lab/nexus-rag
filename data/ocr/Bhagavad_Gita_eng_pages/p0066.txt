Action (with a selfish motive) is far inferior
to this Yoga in the form of equanimity. Do you
seek refuge in this equipoise of mind, Arjuna;
for poor and wretched are those who are
instrumental in making their actions bear
fruit. (49)

Endowed with equanimity, one sheds in
this life both good and evil. Therefore, strive
for the practice of this Yoga of equanimity.
Skill in action lies in (the practice of this)
Yoga.
