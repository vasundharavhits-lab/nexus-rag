Those who perform action with some
interested motive as laid down in these three
Vedas and drink the sap of the Soma plant,
and have thus been purged of sin, worshipping
Me through sacrifices, seek access to heaven;
attaining Indra’s paradise as the result of
their virtuous deeds, they enjoy the celestial
pleasures of gods in heaven. (20)
