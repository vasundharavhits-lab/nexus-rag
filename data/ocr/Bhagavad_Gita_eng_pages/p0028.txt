Arjuna said: Krsna, at the sight of these

kinsmen arrayed for battle my limbs give |

way, and my mouth is parching; nay, a
shiver runs through my body and hair stand
upright. (2nd half of 28 and 29)

The bow, Gandiva, slips from my hand and
my skin too burns all over; my brainis whirling,
as it were, and I can stand no longer. (30)
