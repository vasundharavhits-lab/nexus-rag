The seven great seers, their four elders
(Sanaka and others), and the fourteen Manus
or progenitors of mankind (suchas Svayambhuva
and his successors), who are all devoted to
Me, were born of My will; from them all these
creatures in the world have descended. (6)

He who knows in reality this supreme divine
glory and supernatural power of Mine gets
established in Me through unfaltering Devotion;
of this there is no doubt. (7)
