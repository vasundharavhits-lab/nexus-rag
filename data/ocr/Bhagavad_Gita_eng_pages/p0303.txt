What that Ksetra is and what it is like; and
also what are its evolutes, again, whence is
what, and also finally who that Ksetrajfia is
and what is his glory—hear all this from Me
in a nutshell. (3)

The truth about the Ksetra and the Ksetrajiia
has been expounded by the seers in manifold
ways; again, it has been separately stated in
different Vedic chants and also in the conclusive
and reasoned texts of the Brahmasutras. (4).
