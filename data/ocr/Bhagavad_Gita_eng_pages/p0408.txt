Keenly devoted to his own natural duty,
man attains the highest perfection in the
shape of God-Realization. hear the mode
of performance whereby the man engaged

in his inborn duty reaches that highest
consummation. (45)

Man attains the highest perfection by
worshipping Him through his own natural
duties from whom the tide of creation has

streamed forth and by whom all this universe
is pervaded. (46)
