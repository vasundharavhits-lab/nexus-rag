Of course, the strain is greater for those
who have their mind attached to the Unmanifest;
for atonement with the Unmanifest is attained
with difficulty by those who are centred in
the body. (5)

On the other hand, those who depending
exclusively on Me, and surrendering all actions
to Me, worship Me (God with attributes),
constantly meditating on Me with single-minded
devotion. (6)
