That supreme Brahma is said to be the
light of all lights, and entirely beyond Maya.
that godhead is Knowledge itself, worth
knowing, and worth attaining through real
wisdom, and is particularly seated in the
heart of all. (17)

Thus the truth of the Ksetra and knowledge,
as well as of the object worth knowing, God

has been briefly discussed; knowing this in
reality, My devotee enters into My Being. (18)
