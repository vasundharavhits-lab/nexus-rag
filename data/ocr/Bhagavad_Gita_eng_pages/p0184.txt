Arjuna, of the mightly I am the might, free

| from passion and desire; in beings I am the
sexual desire not conflicting with virtue or
scriptural injunctions. (11)

Whaterever other entities there are, born
of Sattva (the quality of goodness), and those
that are born of Rajas (the principal of activity)
and Tamas (the principal of intertia), know
them all as evolved from Me alone. In reality,
however, neither do I exist in them, nor they
in Me. (12)
