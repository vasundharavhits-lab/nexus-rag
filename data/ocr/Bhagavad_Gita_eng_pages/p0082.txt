two courses of Sadhana (Spiritual discipline)

have been enunciated by Me in the past. In
the case of the Sankhyayogi, the Sadhana
proceeds along the path of Knowledge; whereas
in the case of the Krmayogi, it proceeds along
the path of Action. (3)

Man does not attain freedom from action
(culmination of the discipline of Action) without
entering upon action; nor does he reach
perfection (culmination of the discipline of
Knowledge) merely by ceasing to act. (4)
