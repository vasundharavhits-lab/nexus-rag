Iam the sustainer and ruler of this universe,
its father, mother and grandfather, the one
worth knowing, the purifier, the sacred syllable
OM and the three Vedas-RK, Yajus and
Sama. (17)

I am the supreme goal, supporter, lord,

witness, abode, refuge, wellwisher seeking |

no return, origin and end, resting-place, store-
house (to which all beings return at the time
of universal destruction), and imperishable
seed. (18)
