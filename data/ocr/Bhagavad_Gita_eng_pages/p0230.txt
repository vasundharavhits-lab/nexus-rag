Whosoever offers to Me with love a leaf, a

flower, a fruit or even water, I appear in
person before that disinterested devotee of
sinless mind, and delightfully partake of that
article offered by him with love. (26)

Arjuna, whatever you do, whatever you
eat, whatever you offer as oblation to the
sacred fire, whatever you bestow as a gift,
whatever you do by way of penance, offer it
all to Me. (27)
