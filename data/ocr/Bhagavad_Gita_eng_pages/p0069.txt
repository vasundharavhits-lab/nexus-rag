Arjuna said : Krsna, what is the definition
(mark) of a God-realized soul, stable to
mind and established in Samadhi (perfect
tranquillity of mind)? How does the man of

stable mind speak, how does he sit, how
does he walk? (54)

Sri Bhagavan said: Arjuna, when one |

thoroughly dismisses all cravings of the
mind, and is satisfied in the self through (the
joy of) the self, then he is called stable of
mind. (55)
