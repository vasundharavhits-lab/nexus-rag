He who shrinks not from action which
does not lasting happiness, nor gets attached
to that which is conducive to blessedness,—
imbued with the quality of goodness, he has
all his doubts resolved, is intelligent and a

| man of true renunciation. (10)

Since all actions cannot be given up in
their entirety by anyone possessing a body,
he alone who renounces the fruit of actions is
called a man of renunciation. (11)
