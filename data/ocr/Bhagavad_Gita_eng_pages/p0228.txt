Arjuna, even those devotees who, endowed
with faith, worship other gods (with some

interested motive) worship Me alone, though
with a mistaken approach. (23)

For Iam the enjoyer and also the lord of all
sacrifices; but they know Me not in reality (as

the supreme Deity), hence they fall (i.e., return
to life on earth). (24)
