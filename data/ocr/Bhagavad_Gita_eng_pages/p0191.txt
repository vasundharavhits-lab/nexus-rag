[ The fruit caiheg by these people of small
understanding, however, is perishable. The
worshippers of gods attain the gods; whereas
My devotees, howsoever they worship Me,
eventually come to Me and me alone. (23)

Not knowing My unsurpassable and
undecaying supreme nature, the ignorant believe
Me, the supreme Spirit beyond the reach of
mind and senses, the embodiment of Truth,
Knowledge and Bliss, to have assumed a finite
form through birth (as an ordinary human
being). (24)
