Better is one’s own duty, though devoid of
merit, than the duty of another well-performed;
for performing the duty ordained by his own
nature man does not incur sin. (47)

Therefore, Arjuna, one snould not abandon
one’s innate duty, even though it may be tainted
with blemish; for even as fire is enveloped in
smoke, all undertakings are clouded with

demerit (48)
