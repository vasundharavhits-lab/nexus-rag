The Yogi who is established in union with
Me, and worships Me as residing in all beings
(as their very Self); abides in Me; no matter
what he does. (31)

Arjuna, he who looks on all as one, on the
analogy of his own self, and looks upon the
joy and sorrow of all with a similar eve; —
such a Yogi is deemed the highest of all. (32)
