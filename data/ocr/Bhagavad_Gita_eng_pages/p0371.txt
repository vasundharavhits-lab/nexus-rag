Foods which are bitter, acid, salty, overhot,
pungent, dry and burning, and which cause
suffering, grief and sickness, are dear to the
Rajasika type of men. | (9)

Food which is half-cooked or half-ripe,
insipid, putrid, stale and polluted, and which
is impure too, is dear to men a Tamasika
disposition. (10)
