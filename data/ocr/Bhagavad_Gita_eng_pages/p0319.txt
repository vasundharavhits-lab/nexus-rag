Arjuna, being without beginning and without
attributes, this indstructible supreme Spirit,
though dwelling in the body, in fact does
nothing nor gets contaminated. (31)

As the all-pervading ether is not tainted by
reason of its subtlety, so seated everywhere
in the body, the Self is not affected by the
attributes of the body due to its attributeless
character. (32)
