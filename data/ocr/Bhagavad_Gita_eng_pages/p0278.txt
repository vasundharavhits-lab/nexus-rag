You are the primal Deity, the most ancient
Person; You are the ultimate resort of this
universe. You are both the knower and the
knowable, and the highest abode. It is You
who pervade the universe, assuming endless
forms. (38)

You are Vayu (the wind-god), Yama (the god
of death), Agni (the god of fire), the moon-
god, Brahma (the Lord of Creation), nay, the
father of Brahma Himself. Hail, hail to You a
O You once again. (39)
